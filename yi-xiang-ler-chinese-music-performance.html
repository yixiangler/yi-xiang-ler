About Yi Xiang Ler

Yi Xiang Ler: Bringing Chinese Music Performance to Life in Malaysia

Yi Xiang Ler Chinese Musical Centre (艺乡乐华乐音乐中心) in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, brings the beauty of traditional Chinese music to audiences through education, cultural activities and live performances. With instruments such as the guzheng, erhu, pipa and dizi, the centre celebrates the rich sounds of Chinese musical heritage while creating opportunities for people of different ages to experience and appreciate its artistry.

For event organisers, Yi Xiang Ler offers Chinese music performance services for weddings, corporate dinners, shopping mall celebrations, opening ceremonies and private functions. From an elegant guzheng performance to lively Chinese ensembles and Chinese-Western fusion music, the team can discuss arrangements that suit the event theme and atmosphere. It is also an option for organisers seeking a Chinese New Year performance in Malaysia, where familiar festive melodies and traditional instruments can bring warmth and cultural character to reunions and celebrations.

Beyond live entertainment, Yi Xiang Ler provides music lessons, traditional instrument sales, workshops and Chinese orchestra training. This combination of performance and education gives the centre a distinctive role in keeping Chinese music relevant across generations. Whether you are planning a memorable 华乐表演, introducing children to traditional instruments or exploring a new musical interest yourself, Yi Xiang Ler offers a welcoming place to discover the joy of music. Learn more at https://www.yixiangler.com/.
